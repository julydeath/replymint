01 · How it works

Three seconds from thought to sent.

No windows to open, nothing to copy and paste. ReplyMint lives on a hotkey on Mac and Windows and in a floating bubble on Android — it writes straight into whichever app you're already in.

1
Summon it

On Mac, tap ⌥Space in any app. On Android, tap the mint bubble that appears whenever there's somewhere to type.

2
Say it, naturally

Speak the way you think — half-sentences, corrections, "umm" and all. Or don't say anything: Auto Reply reads the conversation and drafts the answer itself.

3
It's written

A polished, ready-to-send message appears right in the text box — correct names, right tone, proper punctuation. You review it and press send. Always you.

07 · Private by design

Your conversations are not our data.

Every request stands alone. Your words go in, your draft comes out, and the content is gone — we keep an anonymous daily counter, never what you said or wrote.

No content storedMessages, emails, screen text, audio — none of it is saved on our servers. Not logged, not "temporarily retained". Gone when your draft arrives.
Reads only when you askOn Mac it reads the focused window only when you tap the hotkey. On Android it reads the screen only when you tap the bubble. No background monitoring, no keylogging.
Drafts, never sendsOn Android, the app literally contains no code that can press send — an architectural guarantee, not a setting. Every message goes out by your hand.
Mic on your termsThe microphone is live only while you're dictating — tap the hotkey or Stop and it's off. The tray icon always shows when ReplyMint is listening.
